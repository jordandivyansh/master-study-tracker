The 200+ Speech Cadence Protocol (Fixing the Fast Pace)
Daily 45-Minute Evening System (09:00 PM – 09:45 PM)
Core Differentiator
1. The 3-Second Breather
Count "1-1000, 2-1000" before answering any question. Drops adrenaline and prevents rush.
2. 125 WPM Metronome Drill
Read 150 words from an editorial in exactly 65–70 seconds. Enforces deliberate full-stop pauses.
3. Physical Down-Regulation
Drop shoulders by 1 cm, keep back supported, feet flat. Physical calm forces vocal pacing down.

Record a 2-min answer to a DAF probe. Self-audit for filler words ("basically", "so") and eye stability.
